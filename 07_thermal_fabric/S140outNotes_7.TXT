NOTE 1 - Standard 140 / validation project profile
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Program-side assumptions the test specification excludes (daylight saving,
      holidays, automatic loads, latent removal) and the interior solar
      distribution method.

1.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):

1.2.1 Normal (realistic defaults)
        Physical Meaning: Pinocchio-EP's default for design projects:
          weather-file DST and holidays honoured, FullExterior solar
          distribution, ideal loads with constant-SHR dehumidification,
          automatic People/Lights/Equipment/Infiltration from the space type.

1.2.2 Standard 140 / validation: runperiod
        Physical Meaning: RunPeriod: Use Weather File Daylight Saving Period =
          No, Use Weather File Holidays and Special Days = No, Apply Weekend
          Holiday Rule = No; any RunPeriodControl:DaylightSavingTime /
          RunPeriodControl:SpecialDays object is dropped. (5.1.1.1 Time
          convention - "Daylight savings time or holidays shall not be used for
          scheduling.")

1.2.3 Standard 140 / validation: solar
        Physical Meaning: Building > Solar Distribution =
          FullInteriorAndExterior (...WithReflections kept when the Site tab
          turned reflections on). (7.2.1.12 Interior solar distribution -
          programs that calculate it internally shall do so (Table 7-13
          fractions only for programs that cannot); 5.1.1.6 c)

1.2.4 Standard 140 / validation: terrain
        Physical Meaning: Building > Terrain = Country when the project has no
          Site exposure of its own (an explicit Site exposure always wins).
          (7.2.1.1.2.2 / 7.2.1.9.1 - open terrain with scattered obstructions
          (Terrain Category 3) -> EnergyPlus "Country")

1.2.5 Standard 140 / validation: ideal
        Physical Meaning: Every HVACTemplate:Zone:IdealLoadsAirSystem /
          ZoneHVAC:IdealLoadsAirSystem: Dehumidification Control Type = None,
          Humidification Control Type = None, Outdoor Air Method = None (no
          outdoor-air object). (7.2.1.13 a, d and 7.2.1.13.2 e - 100 %
          convective, "no latent heat extraction", "sensible cooling only")

1.2.6 Standard 140 / validation: loads
        Physical Meaning: No automatic People / Lights / ElectricEquipment /
          Infiltration: only the loads the user defines (Internal loads step,
          or model objects) are exported. (7.2.1.6 / 7.2.1.7 (the specified
          infiltration and internal gains are the only ones) and 5.1.1.6
          (non-specified inputs are not permitted))

1.2.7 Standard 140 / validation: outputs
        Physical Meaning: Hourly Output:Variable for Zone Ideal Loads Zone
          Sensible Heating Energy, Zone Ideal Loads Zone Sensible Cooling
          Energy, Zone Mean Air Temperature (key *). (7.3.1, 7.3.2, 7.3.8 -
          annual and hourly heating / sensible cooling loads and zone
          temperatures)

1.2.8 Standard 140 / validation: timestep
        Physical Meaning: Timestep, convergence tolerances and warm-up are NOT
          changed by the profile (the model's own values are used and
          reported). (5.1.1.9 Numerical settings - per the software
          implementation; 5.1.1.4 consistent settings across the suite)

1.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    project.profile = "std140" (Project > Analysis options > Simulation profile
      = Standard 140 / validation) for every Section 7 case. Site exposure is
      not set separately, so the profile's Country terrain (open terrain,
      category 3) applies.


NOTE 2 - Exterior and interior surface heat transfer
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Exterior and interior convective coefficients and longwave radiative
      exchange (7.2.1.9, 7.2.1.10, 7.2.3.1.4.3/4, 7.2.3.2.2, 7.2.3.3.2).

2.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):

2.2.1 Envelope > Surface heat transfer > Inside algorithm: TARP (default) |
  Simple | CeilingDiffuser | AdaptiveConvectionAlgorithm | ASTMC1340
        Physical Meaning: time-step varying interior convection correlations;
          interior longwave exchange is always calculated from the surface
          emittances

2.2.2 Envelope > Surface heat transfer > Outside algorithm: DOE-2 (default) |
  TARP | MoWitt | SimpleCombined | AdaptiveConvectionAlgorithm
        Physical Meaning: time-step varying exterior convection from the
          terrain-corrected wind speed at the surface height and the surface
          roughness; exterior longwave exchange with sky, ground and air from
          the surface emittance

2.2.3 Envelope > Surface heat transfer > constant coefficients by surface type
  / per face
        Physical Meaning: constant convective coefficients (W/m2K) replacing
          the correlations on the chosen surfaces

2.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    Program defaults (time-step varying: TARP inside, DOE-2 outside, calculated
      longwave exchange) for all cases except 450, 460 and 470, which use the
      constant coefficients of Table 7-46 (Block C, note 4), as 7.2.1.9.3 a /
      7.2.1.10.3 a require of programs with time-step varying calculations.


NOTE 3 - Window optical and thermal model
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Window transmission, absorption and conduction (7.2.1.11, 7.2.2.1.6,
      7.2.2.1.7).

3.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):

3.2.1 Window construction: Simple (U-factor, SHGC, VT)
        Physical Meaning: a single equivalent layer derived from whole-window
          performance values

3.2.2 Window construction: Layered: pane by pane
        Physical Meaning: each pane entered with thickness, normal-incidence
          solar/visible transmittance and front/back reflectance, IR
          transmittance, front/back emissivity and conductivity, each gap with
          gas type and thickness; angular optics are calculated from the
          normal-incidence values and gap heat transfer (conduction,
          convection, radiation) each time step with temperature-dependent gas
          properties

3.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    Layered: pane by pane with the Table 7-10 / 7-17 / 7-21 values (the
      builder's Std 140 presets 600 / 660 / 670). Visible-band properties are
      set equal to the solar-band values (they do not affect the thermal
      results).


NOTE 4 - Interior solar distribution
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Distribution of transmitted solar radiation to interior surfaces (7.2.1.12
      and the corresponding sections of 7.2.2 / 7.2.3).

4.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):

4.2.1 FullExterior
        Physical Meaning: transmitted beam solar falls on the floor only

4.2.2 FullInteriorAndExterior
        Physical Meaning: transmitted beam solar is tracked geometrically to
          the interior surfaces it strikes; diffuse and reflected solar is
          distributed by area x solar absorptance; the part reflected back out
          through the windows is lost

4.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    FullInteriorAndExterior (set by the Standard 140 profile): calculated
      internally from the interior solar absorptances, so the alternative
      constant fractions of Tables 7-13, 7-15, 7-30, 7-43, 7-44 and 7-45 are
      not used.


NOTE 5 - Numerical settings
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Time step, conduction solution and initialization (5.1.1.7, 5.1.1.9).

5.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):

5.2.1 Timestep (Number of Timesteps per Hour)
        Physical Meaning: zone heat-balance time step; the program default is 4
          per hour

5.2.2 Heat balance algorithm
        Physical Meaning: conduction transfer functions (default)

5.2.3 Warm-up
        Physical Meaning: the first day of the run period is repeated until
          zone temperatures and loads converge (6 to 25 days) before results
          are recorded (preconditioning per 5.1.1.7)

5.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    Timestep 4 per hour, conduction transfer functions, warm-up 6-25 days,
      convergence tolerances 0.04 (loads) / 0.4 (temperature) (program
      defaults), the same for every case (5.1.1.4). Full annual run periods
      (Jan 1 - Dec 31); hourly outputs integrated by the engine.


NOTE 6 - Weather data and sky temperature
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Use of the normative 725650TY.CSV (7.2.1.1) by an engine that reads EPW.

6.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):

6.2.1 Project > Weather > upload (.epw / TMY3 .csv / .tm2 / .tmy / .wy2)
        Physical Meaning: non-EPW files are converted to EPW by the program's
          weather converter; TMY3 is local standard time, hour-ending, so no
          time shift is applied

6.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    725650TY.CSV converted by the program's converter (pinocchio-wx 1.0.0); the
      converted EPW is archived with the results (weather/725650TY.epw, sha256
      e7404680efbd0aab...). Sky temperature is calculated each hour from the
      horizontal infrared radiation, which the converter derives from dry-bulb,
      dew point and opaque sky cover (Clark-Allen). Ground reflectance: the
      program default 0.2 (Table A1-1).

2.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    7.2.3.1.4, Tables 7-35, 7-36

2.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    Two opaque doors (Geometry > door, 3.0 m x 2.0 m at x = 0.5 m and 4.5 m on
      the south wall, sill 0.2 m) whose construction is the custom opaque
      construction "Std140 HC Element" picked in the door construction select
      (Envelope > + New construction, exact layers): panel 3.048 mm k 1.0 rho
      2470 cp 750; air gap 12.0 mm k 0.0625 rho 1.292498 cp 1006.103271
      (constant); panel 3.048 mm k 1.0 rho 2470 cp 750; roughness VerySmooth;

2.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    Shortwave transmittance is 0 (an opaque door subsurface) and the layers are
      exactly those of Table 7-36; the constant-conductivity gap is entered as
      a solid layer of that conductivity. Geometry, position and area are as
      specified.


NOTE 3 - Night ventilation fan (Cases 650, 950, 650FF, 950FF)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    1700 m3/h vent fan 18:00-07:00 in addition to infiltration, zero fan heat.

3.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    7.2.2.1.5, Table 7-16, 7.2.2.3.2

3.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    A second Internal loads > Infiltration load, method Design Flow Rate,
      0.47222222 m3/s (1700 m3/h), with an hourly fraction schedule (1 for
      00:00-07:00 and 18:00-24:00, else 0; Schedules > Add a schedule).

3.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    The infiltration object is a constant outdoor-air flow (constant term 1, no
      wind or temperature terms) at outdoor density that adds no fan heat: the
      same heat-balance term as a ventilation fan with zero fan heat. The
      engine uses the site barometric pressure, so the sea-level rate of Table
      7-16 (1409 m3/h) is not used.


NOTE 4 - Constant combined surface coefficients (Cases 450, 460, 470)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Table 7-46 combined interior and/or exterior coefficients.

4.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    7.2.3.21-7.2.3.23, Table 7-46

4.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    Envelope > Surface heat transfer > Fill Std 140 Table 7-46 (constant
      coefficients by surface type: walls 1.8 / 21.6, roof 1.7 / 21.8, raised
      floor 3.7 / 5.2, windows 4.5 / 17.8 W/m2K; 460 inside only, 470 outside
      only), entered as convective coefficients, with the IR emittance of the
      affected faces set to 0.00001 (opaque: thermal absorptance of the outer /
      inner layer; windows: outdoor-side emissivity of the outer pane /
      room-side emissivity of the inner pane).

4.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    The program accepts constant convective coefficients; the informative notes
      to 7.2.3.21.1 / 7.2.3.21.2 prescribe entering the combined value as
      convective with the emittance at 0 or the lowest value the program allows
      (the forms require a value > 0).

5.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:

NOTE 6 - Thermostat schedules (Cases 640, 940, 650, 950)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Heating setback with a linear ramp; heating off and cooling only
      07:00-18:00.

6.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    7.2.2.1.4, 7.2.2.1.5

6.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    640/940: heating setpoint schedule entered with + Add object
      (Schedule:Compact, Interpolate: Linear): 10 C to 07:00, ramp to 20 C at
      08:00, 20 C to 23:00, 10 C from the 23:00-23:15 time step on. 650/950:
      heating setpoint -50 C at all hours; cooling setpoint 27 C 07:00-18:00
      and 99 C otherwise (Schedules > Add a schedule; HVAC > zone thermostat
      schedules).

6.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    With 4 time steps per hour the interpolation gives 12.5 / 15 / 17.5 / 20 C
      at the ends of the 07:00-08:00 time steps, as 7.2.2.1.4 requires; the -50
      C / 99 C setpoints are never reached, i.e. the equipment is OFF.

NOTE 1 - Engine psychrometric warnings
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Test Case(s) and Specific Results from the Case(s) that are Anomalous:
    Several cases report one or two run-time warnings "Temperature out of range
      [-100. to 200.] (PsyPsatFnTemp)" / "WetBulb not converged" from the
      wet-bulb routine at isolated time steps (e.g. Case 600: 03/13 10:15 and
      03/14 11:56).

1.2 Explanation of Reason for the Anomalous Results:
    The warnings come from the engine's iterative wet-bulb routine
      (PsyTwbFnTdbWPb) at isolated zone / system time steps. The runs complete
      normally and the hourly loads and zone temperatures around those time
      steps show no discontinuity; the cause was not traced further.
