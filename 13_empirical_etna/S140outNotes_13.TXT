-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      distribution method.

        Physical Meaning: RunPeriod: Use Weather File Daylight Saving Period =

        Physical Meaning: Building > Solar Distribution =
          FullInteriorAndExterior (...WithReflections kept when the Site tab

1.2.5 Standard 140 / validation: ideal
        Physical Meaning: Every HVACTemplate:Zone:IdealLoadsAirSystem /
          ZoneHVAC:IdealLoadsAirSystem: Dehumidification Control Type = None,
          Humidification Control Type = None, Outdoor Air Method = None (no

          convective, "no latent heat extraction", "sensible cooling only")

1.2.6 Standard 140 / validation: loads
        Physical Meaning: No automatic People / Lights / ElectricEquipment /

1.2.7 Standard 140 / validation: outputs
        Physical Meaning: Hourly Output:Variable for Zone Ideal Loads Zone

1.2.8 Standard 140 / validation: timestep
        Physical Meaning: Timestep, convergence tolerances and warm-up are NOT

1.3 Setting or Capability Used:

      dehumidification, humidification or outdoor air, and the hourly
      ideal-load / zone-temperature outputs.


NOTE 2 - Interior surface heat transfer algorithm, Cases ET100A3 and ET100B3
  (13.2.6.4 / 13.2.7.4)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Time-step varying interior convection and interior long-wave radiation
      exchange between the cell surfaces.

2.2 Optional Settings or Modeling Capabilities
    (list setting or capability and its physical meaning):

2.2.1 Inside convection algorithm (Envelope > Surface heat transfer,
  SurfaceConvectionAlgorithm:Inside): Simple, TARP, CeilingDiffuser,
  AdaptiveConvectionAlgorithm, ASTMC1340
        Physical Meaning: Simple: constant natural-convection values by
          orientation. TARP: natural convection by temperature difference and
          tilt. CeilingDiffuser: mixed/forced convection correlations of the
          zone supply air change rate (walls, ceiling, floor).
          AdaptiveConvectionAlgorithm: per-surface selection by flow regime.
          ASTMC1340: attic/horizontal-surface correlations.

2.2.2 Interior long-wave radiation
        Physical Meaning: Exchange between all inside faces with each face's
          thermal emittance (the engine's only interior radiation model; not
          user-selectable).

2.2.3 Per-path constant convection coefficients (Envelope > Surface heat
  transfer > Imported surfaces: SurfaceProperty:ConvectionCoefficients)
        Physical Meaning: Constant convective coefficient per face; used for
          the '1' cases only.

2.3 Setting or Capability Used:
    CeilingDiffuser for both cases, with the interior emittances of Tables
      13-19 / 13-45 restored (0.870 walls and ceiling, 0.846 floor, 0.872 door,
      0.9 glazing, 0.870 frames). Basis: the cell is mechanically mixed by the
      heater-with-diffusers system at about 20.7 / 21.5 ach (four 0.55 m
      diffusers near the floor, intake near the ceiling, 13.2.6.4.1); of the
      program's algorithms only CeilingDiffuser makes the inside coefficients a
      function of the supply air change rate, the others are natural-convection
      or regime-classification correlations that do not see the measured
      airflow. The hourly measured HWD supply air flow (CSV column J,
      designated "Input" in Table 13-3) is imposed as the ideal heater's air
      mass flow (see Block C note 4), so the correlation sees the measured air
      change rate. Interior surface roughness: Smooth (it does not enter the
      inside convection correlations). Other algorithms were run as informative
      sensitivity only; they are listed in the results README, not used for the
      submitted results.

NOTE 1 - Model input route: IDF authored from the normative specification and
  imported
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Test-cell geometry (16 parallel one-dimensional conduction paths), layered
      constructions with imputed conductivities, schedules and the idealized
      heater.

1.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    13.2.2.7, 13.2.2.8 (Tables 13-5 to 13-18), 13.2.3.7 (Tables 13-23 to
      13-37), 13.2.4.7 (Tables 13-42 to 13-44), 13.2.2.14, 13.2.2.16

1.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    Each path is one BuildingSurface:Detailed (net inside area of Tables 13-5 /
      13-23, strips of the 3.5 x 4.655 x 2.565 m cell) with its own
      Construction and Material layers exactly as tabulated (inside layer first
      in the tables, outside layer first in the input). The file was brought in
      through Project > Import IDF together with the schedule CSV; the importer
      inlined the Schedule:File objects as exact Schedule:Compact equivalents.
      Guard boundary conditions, interior coefficients, emittance edits,
      timestep, profile and outputs were then entered in the application (notes
      2, 3, 4).

1.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    The application's footprint/zone editor cannot split one wall, floor or
      ceiling into several parallel conduction paths with different
      constructions; the IDF import route reproduces the specified paths
      exactly. Composite air-to-air R-values computed from the entered layers
      and Table 13-20 coefficients match the informative values of Tables 13-6
      to 13-37 (e.g. Cell A north wall 1.455 vs 1.456, ceiling path 1 6.157 vs
      6.161, floor path 1 2.929 vs 2.928 m2K/W; Cell A UA with insulated
      windows 30.92 W/K = sum of the required UA values).


NOTE 2 - Guard zones as exterior boundary conditions
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Measured guard-zone air temperatures and exterior combined coefficients on
      every bounding surface.

2.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    13.2.2.15.1, 13.2.2.10 (Table 13-20), 13.2.3.12.1, 13.2.4.11.1

2.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    Six SurfaceProperty:OtherSideCoefficients, one per guard zone (attic,
      cellar, north, east, south, west), entered as part of the cell
      description brought in with Project > Import IDF (the program records
      them as the faces' Other side coefficients boundary): Combined
      Convective/Radiative Film Coefficient 18 W/m2K (hcomb,ext), Constant
      Temperature Coefficient 1, all other coefficients 0, Constant Temperature
      Schedule = the measured guard temperature. Every path surface has Outside
      Boundary Condition OtherSideCoefficients with its guard, NoSun, NoWind.

2.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    The other-side coefficient with a positive film coefficient is exactly an
      exterior air temperature with a constant combined coefficient, which is
      the boundary condition 13.2.2.15.1 specifies.


NOTE 3 - Constant combined interior coefficients, Cases ET110A1, ET110B1,
  ET100A1, ET100B1
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Constant interior combined (convective + radiative) surface coefficients of
      Tables 13-20 / 13-39 / 13-46.

3.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    13.2.2.10 Informative Note 4, 13.2.3.9, 13.2.4.9

3.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    A constant inside coefficient per path entered in Envelope > Surface heat
      transfer > Imported surfaces (written as
      SurfaceProperty:ConvectionCoefficients, Inside, Value = hcomb,int) (north
      wall 10.5, door 10.5, ceiling 3.6, floor 5.3, east/south/west walls per
      cell, insulated window paths 7.0 / 7.0 / 12.0, uninsulated window paths
      12.0 W/m2K); the inside layer's Thermal Absorptance set to 0.00001 (the
      program minimum, objects editor field edit).

3.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    The program accepts constant convective coefficients but not combined ones;
      the standard's own instruction (enter hcomb as convective, set the
      interior emittance to 0 or the lowest value allowed) is followed. With
      all inside emittances at the minimum the interior long-wave exchange is
      negligible.


NOTE 4 - Measured schedules, initialization and the measured HWD air flow
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Describe the Effect Being Simulated:
    Hourly measured cell set point, guard temperatures, fan heat and supply air
      flow; data outside the measured period.

4.2 Section(s) of the Standard where Relevant Inputs are Specified:
    13.2.2.2.1 (Table 13-3), 13.2.2.4, 13.2.2.14, 13.2.2.16.2, 13.2.6.4.1

4.3 Equivalent Input(s) Used:
    A schedule CSV (8784 hourly rows, leap year 2000, GMT+1, preceding-hour
      stamps placed on the hour they end) derived from the normative
      measurement CSV: Tattic, Tcellar, Tnorth, Teast, Tsouth, Twest, Tcell,
      Qfan, HWDsafr. Hours before the first measured hour hold the first row,
      hours after the last hold the last row. RunPeriod = the calendar days of
      the measured period (2000), so the program's warm-up repeats the first
      day until converged. Qfan (Wh/h) is an ElectricEquipment of design level
      1 W x the Qfan schedule, fraction radiant 0, latent 0, lost 0 (100 %
      convective). The heater is an ideal loads air system (HVACTemplate)
      heating to the hourly Tcell schedule, unlimited, no outdoor air, cooling
      set point 100 C. The measured HWDsafr (m3/h) is imposed as the heater's
      supply air mass flow with an EMS program (+ Add object: sensor, actuator
      'Ideal Loads Air System / Air Mass Flow Rate', calling manager, program)
      using the intake air density 1.1455 kg/m3 (35 C, 101.325 kPa).

4.4 Physical, Mathematical or Logical Justification of the Equivalent Input(s)
    - provide supporting calculations, if relevant:
    Tables 13-3 marks columns B-J as inputs. The ideal heater stays 100 %
      efficient, convective and unlimited; imposing its flow only gives the
      interior convection correlation of the '3' cases the measured air change
      rate (with constant coefficients the flow has no effect: ET110A1 changes
      by less than 0.01 %). The measured value 1,001.48 in
      ET100A-Measurements.csv (quoted with a thousands separator) is read as
      1001.48.

NOTE 1 - EnergyPlus 26.1: FisherPedersenCeilingDiffuser keys in
  SurfaceProperty:ConvectionCoefficients:MultipleSurface are fatal
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Test Case(s) and Specific Results from the Case(s) that are Anomalous:
    ET100A3 / ET100B3 algorithm options. MultipleSurface AllExteriorWalls
      Inside FisherPedersenCeilingDiffuserWalls (+ Add object): fatal (** Fatal
      error -- final processing. Program exited before simulations began. See
      previous error messages.); MultipleSurface AllExteriorRoofs Inside
      FisherPedersenCeilingDiffuserCeiling (+ Add object): fatal (** Fatal
      error -- final processing. Program exited before simulations began. See
      previous error messages.); MultipleSurface AllExteriorFloors Inside
      FisherPedersenCeilingDiffuserFloor (+ Add object): fatal (** Fatal error
      -- final processing. Program exited before simulations began. See
      previous error messages.).

1.2 Explanation of Reason for the Anomalous Results:
    The calculation engine (EnergyPlus 26.1.0, unmodified) accepts these keys
      in the input dictionary but terminates with 'Unhandled convection
      coefficient algorithm'. The application does not patch the engine; the
      per-surface Fisher-Pedersen correlations are therefore reachable only
      through the zone-level CeilingDiffuser algorithm, which is what Cases
      ET100A3 / ET100B3 use.


NOTE 2 - Cases ET100A3 / ET100B3: sensitivity to the air change rate seen by
  the convection correlation
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Test Case(s) and Specific Results from the Case(s) that are Anomalous:
    Without the measured HWD air flow imposed (the ideal heater's own, much
      smaller, air flow): ET100A3 674.1 Wh/h (-8.48 %), ET100B3 676.0 Wh/h
      (-8.34 %). Other algorithms with the measured flow: ET100A3 TARP -19.2 %,
      ET100A3 AdaptiveConvectionAlgorithm -27.3 %, ET100A3 ASTMC1340 -21.8 %,
      ET100A3 Simple -14.7 %, ET100B3 TARP -19.3 %, ET100B3
      AdaptiveConvectionAlgorithm -27.6 %, ET100B3 ASTMC1340 -22.0 %, ET100B3
      Simple -14.9 %.

2.2 Explanation of Reason for the Anomalous Results:
    The interior coefficients of these cases are driven by forced convection
      from about 21 ach of diffuser air. Correlations that do not use the air
      change rate, or that see only the heat-delivery air flow of an idealized
      heater, under-predict the interior coefficients and therefore the heater
      energy. Reported for transparency; the submitted results use the
      algorithm and settings of Block B.
