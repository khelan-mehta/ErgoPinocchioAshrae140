B. REPORT BLOCK FOR ALTERNATIVE MODELING METHODS
===============================================================================
NOTE 1 - Zone loads
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Effect: fixed zone sensible and latent loads (11.2.1.1.4).
1.2 Options: (1) adiabatic zone with internal gains (negative for heating);
    (2) alternate non-adiabatic zone (11.2.1.1.5).
1.3 Used: option 1. All surfaces adiabatic; sensible cooling loads and latent
    loads as zone equipment gains (fraction radiant 0; latent fraction 1, i.e.
    moisture added at the zone temperature); heating loads as negative
    convective gains.

NOTE 2 - System types
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Effect: FC, SZ, CV terminal reheat and VAV terminal reheat systems.
1.2 Options available: air loop with zone terminals; four-pipe fan-coil zone
    unit (its fan is located upstream of the coils).
1.3 Used: air loop for all four. FC = single-zone air loop with heating coil,
    cooling coil, then supply fan (fan downstream of the coils as in Figure
    11-1), no return fan, CV terminal without reheat.

NOTE 3 - Cooling coil with bypass factor ~0
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Effect: BF = 0 (11.2.1.1.3 d).
1.2 Options: DX coils (bypass factor from rated conditions, cycling); chilled
    water coil (effectiveness model, continuous modulation).
1.3 Used: chilled-water coil with a very large UA (design: 0.01 m3/s water at
    2 C, air 26.7 C -> 4 C), water flow modulated by its controller to the
    leaving-air set point; wet-coil leaving RH 99.3-100 %.

NOTE 4 - Outputs not directly reported by the program
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Specific volume at each node = 287.042 (T+273.15)(1+1.607858 W)/P [m3/kgda]
    (dry-air basis) from the program's node temperature, humidity ratio and
    pressure; enthalpy (J/g) = node enthalpy (J/kg) / 1000; cooling-coil latent
    load = total - sensible; zone latent load = the latent gain rate. Steady
    state is checked as last hour vs previous hour; 24-h medians are also
    recorded (Block G note 1).

C. REPORT BLOCK FOR EQUIVALENT MODELING METHODS
===============================================================================
NOTE 1 - Supply-fan heat
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Effect: fan energy and fan losses to the moved air, motor and drive losses
    outside the airstream (11.2.1.1.1.1 e-h).
1.2 Sections 11.2.1.1.1.1, 11.2.2.1.1.1, 11.2.3.1.1.1, 11.2.4.1.1.1.
1.3 Pressure rise 498 Pa, fan total efficiency 0.7, motor efficiency 1.0,
    motor in airstream fraction 0.
1.4 Heat to air = shaft power = Q x dP / 0.7 = Ho + Ho(1/0.7 - 1), as specified
    (e: fan energy to the air; g: fan losses to the air); motor/drive input is
    not an output.

NOTE 2 - Return fan
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Effect: return fan between zone mixer and OA mixing box, 249 Pa at the design
    return flow, pressure ~ flow^2 (11.2.2.1.1.2, 11.2.3.1.1.2, 11.2.4.1.1.2).
1.3 Fan on the air loop upstream of the outdoor-air system; maximum flow = the
    system supply flow; SZ/CV constant 249 Pa; VAV pressure ~ (flow fraction)^2
    (power coefficients 0,0,0,1,0), total efficiency 0.7, motor outside airstream.
1.4 The program passes the branch (supply) flow through a fan in this position
    and relieves the excess at the mixer; the heat reaching the mixed air is
    Q_supply x dP / 0.7 x (supply - exhaust)/supply = Q_return x dP / 0.7, the
    specified value at design return flow; the fan temperature rise equals the
    specified one. The reported rfi/rfo mass flows are the program's node
    values (supply flow).

NOTE 3 - Ambient conditions
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 Constant hourly weather records (EPW) with the dry-bulb and dew-point of
    Tables 11-4, 11-5, 11-7, 11-14, 11-16, 101325 Pa, 4.3 m/s, no solar
    radiation, entered directly (not the TM2 files).

NOTE 4 - Thermostat without deadband
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.3 Heating and cooling set points both equal to the specified zone temperature
    (dual set point with zero deadband) in every case, per zone.

G. REPORT BLOCK FOR ANOMALOUS RESULTS
===============================================================================
NOTE 1 - VAV cases not exactly steady
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 AE403, AE404, AE405, AE406, AE445: hour-to-hour variation of coil loads and
    node flows of 0.3-1.6 % persists after 5 days of constant conditions.
1.2 Iterative interaction of the VAV terminal damper control and the water-coil
    controller; insensitive to convergence tolerances, HVAC iteration limit and
    timestep. Last-hour values are reported; 24-h medians give the same range
    results.

NOTE 2 - AE301 preheat coil load
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 AE301 preheat 3.550 kWh/h.
1.2 Volumetric flows are converted to mass at standard air density (permitted
    predetermined assumption): outdoor-air mass 1.7 % higher and supply mass
    3.6 % lower than at the specified local conditions, raising the share of
    -29 C outdoor air in the mixed air.

NOTE 3 - Zone temperature offset
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Zone air temperatures 0.005-0.04 K above set point (e.g. AE301 zone 2
    22.261 C vs 22.222 C); no set-point adjustment applied.
